Set up

Ready in three minutes.

Your phone and PC just need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Run FlickDesk on Windows

One file, nothing to install. Allow it on Private networks when Windows Firewall asks. It lives in the system tray.

Install Flick on Android

Open the APK on your phone and allow the install. Android 10 or newer.

Scan the QR code

In Flick, tap +, scan the code on your PC and check the six-digit number matches. That's it.

Private by design

Your files never leave your network.

Local only

Devices find each other on your Wi-Fi and connect directly. Nothing goes through the internet.

Paired with a check number

A one-time QR code and a six-digit number on both screens prove you paired the right device.

End-to-end encrypted

ECDH P-256 key exchange, a fresh key for every connection, and AES-256-GCM for every byte.

FlickDesk for Windows

The tray app: receives flicks and sends files to your phone.

  • Windows 10 (1903+) or 11, 64-bit
  • Single exe; SmartScreen may ask once (More info → Run anyway)
Download FlickDesk-windows-x64.exe

Flick for Android

The phone app: flick photos and files, receive in the background.

  • Android 10 or newer
  • Allow “install unknown apps” when asked
Download Flick-android.apk
